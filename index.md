Privacy Policy for Video mate

Effective Date: October 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Dreamlabs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information in connection with your use of our mobile application, Video mate (the "App").

Information We Collect
We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not collect any personally identifiable information from you, such as your name, address, or phone number. The information we process is limited to what is necessary for the App to function and to improve our services.

a) Information You Provide:

Video URLs: The App's core function is to download videos from URLs that you provide. We only process these URLs to fetch the video content and do not store them after the request is completed.

b) Automatically Collected Information (Non-Personal): To support and enhance the App, we work with third-party services that may collect information automatically.

Google AdMob: Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and other data to provide personalized ads. You can learn more about how Google collects and uses data by visiting their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics Data: We may use analytics services (like Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with our App. This helps us identify bugs and improve the user experience. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

To provide and maintain the core functionality of the App (i.e., downloading videos).

To display advertisements, which helps us keep the App free.

To analyze usage patterns and improve the App's performance and features.

App Permissions
Videomate requires certain permissions to function correctly. Here is a list of the permissions we request and why we need them:

INTERNET: To connect to our backend server, fetch video information, and download files.

WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (on older Android versions): To save downloaded video files to your device's public storage.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show you the live progress of your downloads in the notification bar.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: To ensure that downloads continue reliably in the background, even if you switch to another app.

If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.
